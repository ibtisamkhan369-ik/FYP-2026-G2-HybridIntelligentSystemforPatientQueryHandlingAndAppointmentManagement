# FYP-2026-G2-HybridIntelligentSystemforPatientQueryHandlingAndAppointmentManagement
Hybrid Intelligent System for Patient Query Handling And Appointment Management with guided patient-to-department Routing, Urgency-Based Escalation and Structured Human handoff support
FYP-2026-G2
Ibtisam & Faryal
Problem Statement:
Hospitals and clinics receive a high volume of patient queries regarding appointments, doctor 
availability, timings, and general information. These queries are often handled manually, leading 
to delays, increased staff workload, and inconsistent responses. Existing systems such as manual 
reception or IVR are inefficient and lack flexibility. There is a need for a structured system that 
automates routine interactions while supporting guided routing, urgency-based escalation, and 
human intervention when required.
